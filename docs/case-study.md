# Case Study: Harbor & Pine Realty Lead Automation

## The Pain Point
Real estate agents lose leads when they are busy showing properties or sleeping. A lead that isn't contacted within 5 minutes is 21x less likely to convert. Furthermore, following up manually on day 1, 3, and 7 is tedious and often forgotten, causing potential clients to go cold.

## The Solution
We built a fully automated, self-hosted n8n workflow that:
1. **Instantly captures leads** via a webhook (or form).
2. **Sends a personalized reply** within seconds, tailored to whether the lead wants to Buy, Sell, or Rent.
3. **Alerts the agent** via Telegram immediately.
4. **Nurtures unanswered leads** automatically on Day 1, Day 3, and Day 7.
5. **Monitors itself** and alerts the agent via Telegram if any part of the automation fails.

## The Impact
- **Speed to Lead:** Reduced from hours to < 10 seconds.
- **Agent Time Saved:** ~5 hours/week of manual follow-up and data entry.
- **Conversion Potential:** Ensures no lead is ever forgotten, maximizing the ROI of marketing spend.
